# UX/UI

This app was designed for a specific user. The user is starting a couch to 5K training program and wanted something simple
and easy to use with no signup obligation or fees. User's current method of run interval training involves using her iPhone to
set several alarms to alert her when its time to walk or run. User reports that this slows her down; every time an alarm sounds, 
she has to remove her phone from her pocket and silence the alarm which is difficult while running, and she has to do this repeatedly. 
user also states that she needs something that will be visible in bright sunshine and that apps with a basic white background and black
text are difficult to see when it's bright outside. For this app, I chose a single page interface with tabbed sections that let the user
enter times for warmup, cooldown, intervals, and session duration. There are separate tabs for each phase of the 5K training program. 
The user is alerted to switch between running and walking with distinct alarm sounds; one for run/walk switching, one to let her know when 
she's entering cooldown, and a final alarm to alert her that she has finished the session.
